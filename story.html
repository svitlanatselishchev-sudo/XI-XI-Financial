About

I’m Lana. I make the numbers tie.

Accountant and principal of 11:11 Strategy. I get business owners’ books ready for a buyer, and I specialize in revenue that arrives in more than one currency.

Payouts that don’t match the bank, fees nobody booked, a currency conversion that moved the total: that’s the work I like. I trace it, tie it, and write down whatever is still open, so an owner, a broker or a CPA can rely on what’s left.
