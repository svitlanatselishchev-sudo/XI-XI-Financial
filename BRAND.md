# XI:XI Brand Guide

## What is the “BX” logo design?

“BX” is not a separate company name or abbreviation used by XI:XI. The mark is a **boxed monogram**: a compact logo placed inside a rounded square/box so it remains recognizable at small sizes such as a browser favicon, social avatar, or mobile header.

The monogram represents **XI:XI**:

- The four vertical strokes reference the two “11” forms in the name.
- The center gold dot represents the colon: the point of alignment between the two sides.
- The dark field communicates control, focus, and financial confidence.
- The brass/gold border adds a premium, considered detail without making the identity feel ornamental.

If “BX” was intended to mean **brand experience**, this boxed mark is the brand-experience anchor: it gives the website a consistent visual signature wherever the full wordmark does not fit.

## Logo usage

### Primary wordmark
Use `assets/logo-wordmark.svg` in website headers, footers, presentations, and documents where there is enough horizontal space.

### Compact mark
Use `favicon.svg` for browser tabs, app icons, social avatars, and compact navigation contexts.

### Clear space
Keep approximately one mark-height of breathing room around the logo. Do not place it against busy imagery or low-contrast backgrounds.

### Do not

- Stretch or squash the logo.
- Change the brass accent to another color.
- Add drop shadows directly to the artwork.
- Recreate the monogram with a different typeface.
- Use the compact mark as a replacement for the full wordmark in long-form brand contexts.

## Core palette

- Ink: `#171717`
- Paper: `#F7F4EF`
- Brass: `#B38D5A`
- Brass dark: `#80633D`
- Brass soft: `#D9C39F`
- Muted text: `#686158`

The identity should feel precise, calm, credible, and human—premium through restraint rather than decoration.
