# XI:XI logo clarification

The previous badge was visually ambiguous because its letterforms could be read as “BX.” It has been replaced with an unmistakable **XI:XI** wordmark and compact mark.

The identity now uses:

- Two explicit `XI` groups on either side of the colon.
- A gold colon as the visual alignment point.
- A restrained underline and small `FINANCIAL OPERATIONS` descriptor in the full wordmark.
- A compact black-and-brass square mark for the favicon and small digital placements.

This is intentionally a wordmark-first identity: the viewer should read **XI:XI** immediately, rather than decode an abstract monogram.
