For CPA Firms

A place to send the work you don't have time for.

Cleanup, reconciliation, and messy-books projects eat hours that aren't worth billing at your rate — especially right before tax season. I take that work off your plate two different ways, depending on what fits your firm.

Option 1

Hand me the overflow.

When a cleanup or reconciliation project is more than your team can staff, I take it on as your subcontractor. Your client's export goes to me — never a login, never direct access to their live software — and I diagnose the problem, build the fix, and hand it back to you. You review it and deliver it to your client under your own name. I stay entirely behind the scenes.

  • No access to your client's bank login or accounting software required
  • You keep the client relationship end to end
  • Fixed-fee, scoped per project — no surprises
Send Me Overflow Work
Option 2

Refer a client, earn a share.

If a client's books are too messy for the scope of your engagement — or it's simply not a good use of your time — send them my way. I take it from there: I bill the client directly under my own name, and you earn a referral percentage once the engagement closes. No paperwork on your end beyond a short referral agreement, and no obligation to send anyone unless it's a genuine fit.

  • You earn a percentage of the completed engagement
  • I handle the client relationship and delivery entirely
  • A simple, one-page referral agreement — no long-term commitment

Exact terms — project scope, timeline, and referral percentage — are worked out on a short call, based on the client or project in front of us.

How it works, either way

1 You send over the client's exported statements — no login, no software access needed.
2 I diagnose what's wrong and write it up in plain language — what I checked, what I found, what I recommend.
3 I build the fix — a written procedure, a custom Excel system, or both — and hand it back, delivered your way.

I'm not a CPA and I don't do bookkeeping — no audits, attest work, tax return preparation, payroll tax filing, or ongoing books work of any kind. I diagnose and fix what's already broken in the numbers, then hand it back, so your team can focus on the work only a CPA firm can do.

I diagnose what's off across your books — from reconciliation to cross-border — then build the fix that makes it stick.
