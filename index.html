Questions

Before you book.

Do you prepare tax returns or file sales tax?

No. I don’t prepare tax returns, file sales tax or give tax or legal advice. Your CPA handles the tax work; I make sure the books underneath it are right.

What does it cost?

Every project is quoted after a call, once I know the months, the sales channels and the state of your books. You see the full scope in writing before work starts.

How far back do you go?

12, 24 or 36 months, depending on what your buyer will ask for.

I’m afraid of what you’ll find.

Most projects start messy. Bring the books as they are; anything that doesn’t tie gets written down, not hidden.

Do you do monthly bookkeeping?

No. My work is project-based: Sale-Ready Books, tie-outs and cleanups. For multi-entity, multi-currency companies I also run a monthly close.

Which languages do you work in?

English, Ukrainian and Russian.
