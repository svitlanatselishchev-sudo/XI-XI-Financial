Questions

Before you book.

Do you prepare tax returns or file sales tax?

No. I don’t prepare tax returns, file sales tax for clients, make tax payments, or give tax or legal advice. I’m an accountant, not an attorney or CPA. Your CPA handles the tax work; I get the books underneath it right.

What does it cost?

Every project is quoted after a call, once I know the months, the sales channels and the state of your books. The 15-minute intro call is free, and you see the full scope in writing before any work starts.

How far back do you go?

Sale-Ready Books covers 12, 24 or 36 months. We pick the period on the call, based on what your buyer is likely to ask for.

I’m afraid of what you’ll find.

Most projects start from messy. You don’t need to tidy anything first. Bring the books as they are; anything that doesn’t tie gets written down, not hidden.

Do you do monthly bookkeeping?

No. My work is project-shaped: Sale-Ready Books, tie-outs and cleanups. For multi-entity, multi-currency companies I also run a monthly close.

Which languages do you work in?

English, Ukrainian and Russian, for calls, walkthroughs and workshops.
