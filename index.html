More answers on Services.

Do you prepare my tax returns?
No. I don't prepare income tax returns or give tax advice. I can register for and file sales tax returns, and schedule estimated tax payments using the amounts your CPA approves.
Can you help with budgets and cash forecasts?
Yes. Once your books close cleanly each month, I can add a budget, a 13-week cash-flow forecast and scenario models to your monthly engagement, so you plan from numbers you trust.
My CPA already handles this.
Your CPA prepares your return. I fix the books underneath it and work alongside them, so year-end often goes faster.
I'm afraid of what I'll find.
Most cleanup starts from messy. You don't need to tidy anything first. Bring the books as they are.
Which languages do you work in?
English, Russian and Ukrainian, for calls, walkthroughs and workshops.
