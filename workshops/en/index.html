Questions

The short answers

English or Ukrainian?

Each workshop runs separately in English and separately in Ukrainian, on different dates. We never mix the two.

Is this for all of Florida?

Yes. State rules are the same everywhere; local rules depend on your city or county. Times are shown in Eastern and Central.

Is this advice?

No. It is general educational information. For your own case, ask an attorney and a CPA.

What do I need to bring?

A laptop or phone and your own numbers. If documents are needed, we tell you ahead of time. Never share a login.
