Agenda

What we do

  • Price and idea check: your minimum price and how many sales cover your costs.
  • Sole proprietorship vs. LLC, the money side: what each costs to set up and keep, and how owner pay is generally treated. You leave with questions for your attorney and CPA. The choice itself is theirs.
  • Launch steps at a glance: a general overview of the registrations new Florida businesses commonly meet (name, EIN, bank account, sales tax registration, local permit), where the official forms are, and who handles each. You complete your own filings.
  • The local permit: where to look, using a Martin County example.
  • Five questions to ask your city and your HOA.
  • Dates and penalties that are easy to miss.
  • Your first 90 days of costs.
Result

What you take home

A launch checklist, a questions-for-your-attorney sheet and a 90-day cost sheet.

Who it is for

For anyone thinking about a business in Florida, or who has started and wants to check nothing is missing.

Please note

I am an accountant, not an attorney or a CPA. This workshop is general education about the financial side of starting a business. It does not include legal advice, choosing a business structure for you, or preparing or filing formation documents. Those are for a Florida-licensed attorney. Tax filings are for a CPA or tax professional.

This workshop is taught in English. The Ukrainian version runs separately, on a different date.
