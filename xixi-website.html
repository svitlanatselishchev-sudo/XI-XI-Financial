How it works.

Four steps. No standalone reports at any point in the sequence.

01

You send statements, not credentials.

CSV or PDF exports — bank statements, subscription lists, vendor invoices. No live bank login, no API pull, no third-party sync tool granted access to your accounts. If it can't be exported as a file, I don't touch it.

02

I surface the top three.

Every review turns up more than three issues. I don't hand you all of them. Findings are ranked by recovery size and cut to the three that matter most this cycle — because a list of twenty gets filed away, and a list of three gets done.

Capped at 3 per cycle
03

I fix it live, with you.

The deliverable is a working session, not an inbox attachment. I pull up the actual accounts and act — cancel the subscription, draft the vendor call, adjust the invoice term — while you're in the room.

No email-only delivery
04

I check the bank, not my notes.

Thirty days out, I pull the updated statement and confirm the recovered capital actually landed. That verification is what a case study is built on — not what I found, what showed up in the account.

30-day checkpoint, mandatory

The hard rules.

Boundaries I don't move for a bigger engagement or an easier sale.

Not a CPA or accounting firm.

I don't prepare, compile, or review financial statements, and I don't provide audit, tax, or assurance services of any kind. If a finding touches something that needs a CPA or an attorney, I say so and step aside.

Zero-trust data handling.

I work only from the records and statements you provide — no live bank connections, no automated sync, no standing access to an account. Every figure that reaches a working session has been manually verified, line by line, against your original source document. Nothing is trusted until I've checked it by hand.

Active recovery, not a report.

A finding that isn't implemented isn't worth anything. Every engagement ends in a live session where the fix happens on the spot — cancellation, renegotiation, or a corrected term — not a PDF for someone to action later.

Scope stops at what you gave me.

My analysis is limited strictly to the records and statements you provide. I don't make representations about your business beyond that scope, and I don't extend findings into areas — legal, tax, investment — outside operational strategy.
