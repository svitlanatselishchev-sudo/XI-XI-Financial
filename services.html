Questions

Good to know.

What does it cost?

Every project is quoted after a call, once I know the months, the sales channels and the state of your books. You see the full scope in writing before work starts.

Do you prepare tax returns or file sales tax?

No. I don’t prepare tax returns, file sales tax for clients, make tax payments, or give tax or legal advice. I’m an accountant, not an attorney or CPA. Your CPA handles the tax work; I get the books underneath it right.

How do you get my files?

Once scope is agreed, you get a secure file request. Work starts when everything is in, so nothing stalls halfway.

Can you do this without a monthly contract?

Yes. Sale-Ready Books, Tie-Outs, cleanups and app sale packs are one-time projects with a defined finish line.

Which languages do you work in?

English and Ukrainian.
