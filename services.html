What does the first call cost?
Nothing. It is 15 minutes, by video or phone, and ends with a clear yes or no and a next step. The paid session ($125, 60 minutes) is where we go through your books and you get a fixed quote.
Do you prepare or file my taxes?
No. I do not prepare or file tax returns. I organize, reconcile and restructure your books so your CPA or tax preparer can file with confidence.
How do you get my records?
Exported files are preferred. If logging in is easier, you add me as an invited user, so you never share a password.
How does payment work?
You get a written scope and fixed price before anything is billed. Projects are 50% to start and 50% on delivery.
Which languages do you work in?
English, Russian and Ukrainian, for sessions, walkthroughs and workshops.
