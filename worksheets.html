California Sales Tax Worksheet

One workbook. Every California sale, filed right.

The California Sales Tax Worksheet is a ready-to-use Excel workbook for California sellers who want to stop second-guessing every sale. Built for sellers who file in California — and for the CPAs who review their work.

What it does

  • Tells you whether a sale is taxable, exempt or out of scope — before you file.
  • Calculates the correct California sales tax on every line.
  • Separates district tax so you never overpay a jurisdiction you do not owe.
  • Flags marketplace sales already collected by the facilitator.
  • Produces a CDTFA-ready summary you can hand to your CPA.

Inside the workbook

  • Sales Input — enter each sale once; tax, district and exemption logic apply automatically.
  • CDTFA Summary — totals match the return lines, ready to file.
  • District Rates — California district rates, kept current.
  • Exemption Guide — the exemptions California sellers actually use, with the conditions that apply.
