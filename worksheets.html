What it does

  • Multi-State Calculator: tax, vendor discount, amount due and due date for all 12 states side by side (one row per state, using your average local rate; for city-by-city detail use the single-state worksheets)
  • Must I Collect + Nexus Data: type 24 months of sales into each state and read a plain result for each (met, close, or below), using the rule each state’s own page gives
  • Filing Calendar: every return, every state, the whole year, plus your next due date
  • Tax Owed Rollforward: what you owe each state at period end, checked against your balance sheet
  • Marketplace Recon: checks that Etsy, Amazon, eBay and similar payouts add up
  • Registration Tracker: where you are registered, account numbers, who files
  • Exemption Certificates: resale and exemption certificates, with missing and expiring flags
  • Taxability Matrix: your own sheet for what is taxable in each state
  • State Dashboard and State Details: one-page summary and every source

Who it is for

Sellers, bookkeepers and accountants who handle sales tax in several states and want one organized, checked file. Built for sales you handle yourself (your own website, in-person sales, invoices and other channels) and for keeping track of marketplace sales.

The toolkit keeps marketplace sales out of the tax you owe and still lets you track them, because some states count them toward the out-of-state line.

Who it is for

Small business owners, side-hustle sellers, bookkeepers and accountants who sell into or in California and want a clear, checked worksheet instead of a blank spreadsheet. It is built for sales you handle yourself: your own website, in-person sales, invoices and other channels.

Please read: marketplaces. Etsy says it collects and remits sales tax on Etsy orders in this state, so tax on those orders is handled by Etsy. The worksheet has a column for marketplace sales so they are kept out of the tax you owe. Some states still count marketplace sales toward the out-of-state line, and the worksheet shows what each state’s own page says.

How it works

  1. Open the file and read the Start Here tab (two minutes).

What is inside (6 working tabs plus a guide)
