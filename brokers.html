What lands on your desk

  • Every sales-channel payout tied to a bank deposit
  • 12, 24 or 36 months reconciled, chosen with the owner
  • A written list of open questions, with months and amounts
  • Plain-language answers in English, Ukrainian or Russian

Why it helps

Online sellers have messy money trails.

Payouts net out fees and refunds, land days later, and sometimes arrive in another currency. I untangle that before a buyer starts asking.

Payouts that tie

Every payout from Shopify, Amazon, Etsy, Stripe or app stores matched to the bank.

The period buyers ask about

12, 24 or 36 months, reconciled month by month.

Questions answered in writing

Anything open is written down with the month and amount, not left for diligence to find.
